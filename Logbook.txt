Weekly Reflection :
This week brought together string matching, simulation, sorting, and graph reasoning.

DifferentStrings showed how a seemingly combinatorial modification task simplifies to discrete alignment checks.
BigBurger demonstrated how time dependencies can be simulated linearly with a running variable.
BridgeSort reinforced categorical and hierarchical sorting through structured grouping.
LostParentheses extended the theme of simplifying seemingly combinatorial problems.
In HandsShaking, identifying that every handshake divides the circle into independent subproblems, I transformed a potentially exponential search into an elegant dynamic programming formulation.

DifferentStrings: Continuous edits can often be replaced by discrete sliding comparisons.
BigBurger: Sequential time tracking models queues efficiently without explicit data structures.
BridgeSort: Hierarchical sorting by suit and value clarifies multi-key ordering.
LostParentheses: Parentheses optimization can reduce to partitioning logic instead of exhaustive search
HandsShaking: Recognizing symmetry and recursion in visual setups simplifies reasoning and computation.
